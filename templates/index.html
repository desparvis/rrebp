About The Platform

RREBP serves as the definitive administrative infrastructure for real estate governance in Rwanda. By utilizing advanced database verification, we provide a single source of truth for property owners, brokers, and governing bodies.

Our platform allows regional and national associations to manage their respective members autonomously, while maintaining strict, top-level compliance standards overseen by platform administrators. This approach protects against fraudulent practices and elevates the professional standard of the Rwandan real estate market.
